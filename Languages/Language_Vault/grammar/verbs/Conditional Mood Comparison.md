# Conditional Mood — Cross-Linguistic Comparison

## How to Say "I would..." in Each Language

### Czech
**bych/bys/by/bychom/byste/by + l-participle (past tense form)**

| Person | Conditional | Example: dělat (to do) |
|--------|-------------|----------------------|
| já | bych | dělal/a **bych** |
| ty | bys | dělal/a **bys** |
| on/ona | by | dělal/a **by** |
| my | bychom | dělali **bychom** |
| vy | byste | dělali **byste** |
| oni | by | dělali **by** |

### Polish
**by + personal ending attached to past tense**

| Person | Conditional | Example: robić (to do) |
|--------|-------------|----------------------|
| ja | bym | robi**łbym** / robi**łabym** |
| ty | byś | robi**łbyś** / robi**łabyś** |
| on/ona | by | robi**łby** / robi**łaby** |
| my | byśmy | robili**byśmy** |
| wy | byście | robili**byście** |
| oni | by | robili**by** |

### German
**würde + infinitive** (or Konjunktiv II for common verbs)

| Person | würde form | Common verb form |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|
| ich | **würde** machen | hätte, wäre, könnte, müsste |
| du | **würdest** machen | hättest, wärst |
| er/sie | **würde** machen | hätte, wäre |
| wir | **würden** machen | hätten, wären |
| ihr | **würdet** machen | hättet, wärt |
| sie | **würden** machen | hätten, wären |

### Russian
**бы + past tense form (anywhere in sentence)**

| Person | Conditional | Example: делать |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| я | бы | я дела**л/а бы** |
| ты | бы | ты дела**л/а бы** |
| он/она | бы | он/она дела**л/а бы** |
| мы | бы | мы дела**ли бы** |
| вы | бы | вы дела**ли бы** |
| они | бы | они дела**ли бы** |

## Comparison Summary

| Feature | 🇨🇿 | 🇵🇱 | 🇩🇪 | 🇷🇺 |
|---------|------|------|------|------|
| Particle | by- (separate) | -by- (attached!) | würde (verb) | бы (separate) |
| Combined with | past tense | past tense | infinitive | past tense |
| Position | 2nd position (clitic) | suffix on verb | V2 position | flexible |
| Gender marked? | Yes (l-participle) | Yes | No! | Yes |

> **CZ and RU are most similar:** both use a separate particle (by/бы) + past tense.
> **PL is unique:** the "by" fuses INTO the verb form as a suffix (-bym, -byś, etc.)
> **DE is simplest:** würde + infinitive, no gender marking

## "If..." Clauses

### Real condition (If X happens, Y will happen)
| Language | Pattern | Example |
|----------|---------|---------|
| CZ | Jestli/Pokud + present, future | **Jestli** přijdeš, **budu** rád. |
| PL | Jeśli/Jeżeli + present, future | **Jeśli** przyjdziesz, **będę** zadowolony. |
| DE | Wenn + present, present/future | **Wenn** du kommst, **bin** ich froh. |
| RU | Если + present/future, future | **Если** придёшь, я **буду** рад. |

### Unreal condition (If X happened, Y would happen)
| Language | Pattern | Example |
|----------|---------|---------|
| CZ | Kdybych + past, conditional | **Kdybych** měl čas, **šel bych**. |
| PL | Gdybym + past, conditional | **Gdybym** miał czas, **poszedłbym**. |
| DE | Wenn + Konj. II, würde/Konj. II | **Wenn** ich Zeit **hätte**, **würde** ich gehen. |
| RU | Если бы + past, бы + past | **Если бы** у меня **было** время, я **пошёл бы**. |

> **CZ kdyby- = PL gdyby-** = "if-would" fused into one word! Same structure!
> CZ kdybych = PL gdybym, CZ kdybys = PL gdybyś — almost identical forms!
> **DE** sends the verb to the END of the wenn-clause (subordinate clause rule).
> **RU** uses если бы — the "бы" floats freely.
