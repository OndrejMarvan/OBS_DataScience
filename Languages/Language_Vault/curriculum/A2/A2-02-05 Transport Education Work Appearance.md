|---|------|------|------|------|------|-------|
| 1 | auto | samochód/auto | Auto | машина/автомобиль | car | PL samochód = "self-goer" |
| 2 | autobus | autobus | Bus | автобус | bus | international |
| 3 | vlak | pociąg | Zug | поезд | train | all different! |

| 9 | nádraží | dworzec | Bahnhof | вокзал | train station | all different |
| 10 | zastávka | przystanek | Haltestelle | остановка | stop | CZ+PL: from "zastavit/zatrzymać" |
| 11 | jízdenka | bilet | Fahrkarte | билет | ticket | PL+RU bilеt from French |

> **"Airplane" etymology:** PL samolot = RU самолёт = "self-flyer". CZ letadlo from "letět" (fly). DE Flugzeug = "fly-thing". EN airplane = "air-plane". Each language invented its own word!
