# B2-10 Idioms and Register

## Formal vs Informal Register
| Context | 🇨🇿 | 🇵🇱 | 🇩🇪 | 🇷🇺 |
|---------|------|------|------|------|
| "you" formal | Vy (+ 2nd pl) | Pan/Pani (+ 3rd sg!) | Sie (+ 3rd pl) | Вы (+ 2nd pl) |
| "you" informal | ty | ty | du | ты |
| Greeting formal | Dobrý den | Dzień dobry | Guten Tag | Здравствуйте |
| Greeting informal | Ahoj/Čau | Cześć | Hallo/Hi | Привет |
| "please" | prosím | proszę | bitte | пожалуйста |
| Apology formal | Omlouvám se | Przepraszam | Entschuldigen Sie | Извините |

> ⚠️ **Polish formality is UNIQUE:** Pan/Pani uses 3RD PERSON singular, not 2nd person!
> "Czy Pan mówi po angielsku?" = literally "Does Sir speak English?"
> This is MORE formal than CZ Vy, DE Sie, or RU Вы — Polish is the most elaborate formal system of all five.
