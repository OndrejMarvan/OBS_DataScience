# B2 — Upper Intermediate (Vantage)

## Goal
Understand the main ideas of complex text on both concrete and abstract topics, including technical discussions in your field. Interact with a degree of fluency and spontaneity. Produce clear, detailed text on a wide range of subjects and explain a viewpoint.

**Vocabulary target:** ~4500 words (cumulative)
**Duration:** ~6-8 months

### Cross-linguistic transfer notes for B2
> **Register and formality:** Polish Pan/Pani system ≈ German Sie/du ≈ Czech Vy/ty. Russian has ты/вы. The concept transfers, but the social rules differ (Polish is the most formal of the four).
> **Academic vocabulary:** At B2, you'll notice massive Latin/Greek cognate overlap across all five languages — words like "demokracja/Demokratie/демократия/demokracie/democracy" are nearly free.
> **Idioms:** This is where languages diverge most — map Polish idioms to Czech equivalents, note which ones exist in German/Russian too.
