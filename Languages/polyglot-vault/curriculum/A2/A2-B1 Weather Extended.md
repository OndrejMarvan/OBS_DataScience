> **Thunder:** CZ hrom = PL grzmot = RU гром — CZ h↔PL grz↔RU г pattern!
> **Frost:** CZ mráz = PL mróz = RU мороз — all cognate! RU adds the "oro" expansion (полногласие).
> **Sky:** CZ nebe = PL niebo = RU небо — identical concept and root!
> **Dew:** CZ rosa = PL rosa = RU роса — identical across all three!
> **Fog:** CZ mlha = PL mgła — cognate! Both from Proto-Slavic *mьgla.
> **Cloud:** CZ oblak = RU облако — cognate! PL chmura is different root.
> **Muggy:** CZ dusno = PL duszno — cognate! From "dusit/dusić" (to suffocate/stew).
