> **Raspberry:** CZ malina = PL malina = RU малина — identical across all three!
> **Cherry:** CZ třešně = PL czereśnia = RU черешня — cognate (sweet cherry). CZ višně = PL wiśnia = RU вишня (sour cherry) also cognate!
> **Pear:** CZ hruška = PL gruszka — h↔g pattern again!
> **Cabbage:** PL kapusta = RU капуста — identical! CZ zelí is from different root (German Kohl region).
> **Quark/curd:** CZ tvaroh = PL twaróg = RU творог — cognate! And DE Quark was BORROWED from Slavic tvaroh/twaróg!
> **Ice cream:** CZ zmrzlina (from "zmrzlý"=frozen), PL lody (from "lód"=ice), RU мороженое (from "мороз"=frost). All from "cold/frozen" but different roots.
> **Cucumber:** PL ogórek = RU огурец = DE Gurke — all from same Greek/Byzantine root! DE Gurke borrowed FROM Slavic.
