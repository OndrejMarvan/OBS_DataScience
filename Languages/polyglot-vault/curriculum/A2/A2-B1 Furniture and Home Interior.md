> **Fridge:** CZ lednička = PL lodówka — both from "ice" (led/lód). DE Kühlschrank = "cool-cabinet." RU холодильник from "холод" (cold). Same concept, different roots.
> **Oven:** CZ trouba = PL piekarnik (from "piec"=bake) = DE Backofen (back=bake) = RU духовка (from "дух"=spirit/steam). Each language's own etymology!
> **Drawer:** CZ šuplík from DE Schublade! PL szuflada also from DE! RU ящик is native Slavic.
> **Tap/faucet:** CZ kohoutek = literally "little rooster" (the handle shape). PL+RU кран from German/Dutch "Kran" (crane). EN faucet from French.
> **Pillow:** CZ polštář ≠ PL poduszka = RU подушка. PL+RU cognate! CZ polštář from different root.
