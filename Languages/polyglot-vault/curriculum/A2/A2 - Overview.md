# A2 — Elementary (Waystage)

## Goal
Understand sentences and frequently used expressions related to areas of most immediate relevance (personal info, shopping, local geography, employment). Communicate in simple, routine tasks.

**Vocabulary target:** ~1200 words (cumulative)
**Duration:** ~3-4 months

## Grammar — A2

### Core Grammar Points
1. [[Cases - Cross-Linguistic Comparison|All Seven Cases Overview]] — nominative through vocative, when to use each
2. [[Cases - Cross-Linguistic Comparison|Genitive Case]] — possession, negation, quantities, prepositions (do, z, od, bez, dla)
3. [[Cases - Cross-Linguistic Comparison|Dative Case]] — indirect objects, prepositions (ku, dzięki, przeciw)
4. [[Cases - Cross-Linguistic Comparison|Instrumental Case]] — with/by, prepositions (z, nad, pod, przed, za, między)
5. [[Cases - Cross-Linguistic Comparison|Locative Case]] — prepositions (w, na, o, po, przy)
6. Past Tense — -ł/-ła/-ło/-li/-ły endings, gender in past tense
7. Future Tense — będę + infinitive / będę + ł-form
8. [[Aspect Comparison|Verb Aspect Introduction]] — perfective vs imperfective (dokonany/niedokonany)
9. Modal Verbs — musieć, móc, chcieć, potrzebować, umieć
10. [[Adjective Comparison|Comparison of Adjectives]] — -szy/-iejszy, bardziej, naj-
11. [[B1 Reflexive Verbs Comparison|Reflexive Verbs]] — się verbs
12. [[Prepositions Comparison|Prepositions with Cases]] — which preposition governs which case
13. Conjunctions — że, bo, ale, więc, albo, gdy, kiedy, jeśli

### Cross-linguistic transfer notes for A2
> **From Czech:** Case system is your superpower. Czech 7 cases → Polish 7 cases, largely the same logic. Past tense gender agreement works identically. Aspect system is the same concept (dokonavý/nedokonavý → dokonany/niedokonany).
> **For German later:** German only has 4 cases (Nom, Acc, Dat, Gen) — think of it as a simplified version of what you already know.
> **For Russian later:** Russian has 6 cases (no vocative in modern use). Almost identical to Polish system.
