> **Gym:** CZ posilovna (from "síla" = strength), PL siłownia (from "siła" = strength) — same concept!
> **Muscles:** CZ svaly ≠ PL mięśnie ≠ RU мышцы. But PL mięśnie and RU мышцы are both from "myš/mysz/мышь" (mouse!) — muscles looked like mice under skin.
