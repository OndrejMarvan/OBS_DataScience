> **Wolf:** CZ vlk = PL wilk = RU волк = DE Wolf = EN wolf — ALL cognate from PIE!
> **Bear:** CZ medvěd = PL niedźwiedź = RU медведь — all mean "honey-eater" (med+věd)! A euphemism because the original PIE word for bear was taboo.
> **Goose:** CZ husa = PL gęś = DE Gans = RU гусь = EN goose — ALL from PIE *ǵʰh₂éns. CZ h↔PL g pattern!
> **Mouse:** CZ myš = PL mysz = DE Maus = RU мышь = EN mouse — ALL from PIE *mūs. One of the oldest shared words.
> **Rabbit:** CZ králík = PL królik = RU кролик — from "král/król" (king)! Literally "little king" — named for the white fur resembling royal ermine.
> **Butterfly:** CZ motýl = PL motyl — cognate! But DE Schmetterling, RU бабочка (from "бабка"=old woman), EN butterfly — all completely different and creative!
