> **In-law complexity in Russian:** RU distinguishes wife's father (тесть) vs husband's father (свёкор), wife's mother (тёща) vs husband's mother (свекровь), wife's brother (шурин) vs husband's brother (деверь). CZ+PL+DE+EN don't make these distinctions!
> **Nephew:** PL distinguishes siostrzeniec (sister's son) vs bratanek (brother's son). CZ+DE+EN don't.
> **Cousin:** PL kuzyn/kuzynka (from French), RU uses descriptive phrase "двоюродный брат/сестра" (second-degree brother/sister).

> **To get married (gender-specific!):**
> CZ: muž se **žení** (man marries), žena se **vdává** (woman marries)
> PL: mężczyzna się **żeni**, kobieta **wychodzi za mąż** (woman "goes behind husband")
> RU: мужчина **женится**, женщина **выходит замуж** (same as PL!)
> DE+EN: just "heiraten/marry" — no gender distinction in the verb!
> **Childhood:** CZ dětství = PL dzieciństwo = RU детство — all cognate from "dítě/dziecko/дитя" (child)
> **Old age:** CZ stáří = PL starość = RU старость — all from "starý/stary/старый" (old)
> **Death:** CZ smrt = PL śmierć = RU смерть — cognate! All from PIE *mr̥tis. Also related to DE Mord, EN murder.
