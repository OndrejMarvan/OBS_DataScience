> **Office:** CZ kancelář = from Latin "cancellaria". PL biuro = DE Büro = from French "bureau". RU офис = from EN "office". Four different borrowing sources!
> **Meeting:** CZ schůzka/porada = PL spotkanie/zebranie — different words! CZ porada = advisory meeting, PL zebranie = gathering.
> **Stamp/seal:** CZ razítko = PL pieczątka = RU печать — PL+RU cognate (pieczątka/печать from "pečeť/pieczęć"). CZ razítko from "razit" (to stamp/mint).
