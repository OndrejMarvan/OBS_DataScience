> **Linden:** CZ lípa = PL lipa = RU липа — identical! The national tree of Slavic countries. Czech "Lipová alej," Polish "Pod Lipami," German "Unter den Linden" (Berlin) — all the same tree!
> **Oak:** CZ dub = PL dąb = RU дуб — cognate! Polish has nasal ą.
> **Birch:** CZ bříza = PL brzoza = RU берёза — cognate! (The month "březen/marzec" relates to birch in Czech.)
> **Root:** CZ kořen = PL korzeń = RU корень — cognate!
> **Leaf:** CZ list = PL liść = RU лист — cognate! Also means "letter/sheet" in all three.
> **Bark:** CZ kůra = PL kora = RU кора — cognate! CZ kůra also means "crust" (of bread).
> **Poppy:** CZ mák = PL mak = RU мак — identical!
