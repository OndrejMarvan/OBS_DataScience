> **Bake:** CZ péct = PL piec = RU печь — all cognate! From Proto-Slavic *pekti. Also gives us CZ pec/PL piec/RU печь = "oven/stove"
> **Salt:** CZ sůl = PL sól = RU соль = DE Salz = EN salt — ALL from Latin "sal". Universal word!
> **Honey:** CZ med = PL miód = RU мёд — cognate! DE Honig + EN honey are from different root but cognate with each other.
> **Mushroom:** CZ houba ≠ PL grzyb = RU гриб. PL+RU cognate, CZ uses different word.
