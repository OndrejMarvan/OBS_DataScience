## Materials
| 🇨🇿 | 🇵🇱 | 🇩🇪 | 🇷🇺 | 🇬🇧 |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| bavlna | bawełna | Baumwolle | хлопок | cotton |
| vlna | wełna | Wolle | шерсть | wool |
| hedvábí | jedwab | Seide | шёлк | silk |
| kůže | skóra | Leder | кожа | leather |
| len | len | Leinen | лён | linen |

> **Cotton:** DE Baumwolle = "tree-wool". CZ bavlna = PL bawełna — cognate!
> **Wool:** CZ vlna = PL wełna — cognate! DE Wolle + EN wool are also cognate.
> **Leather/skin:** CZ kůže = RU кожа — cognate! Also means "skin" in both.
> ⚠️ **DE Rock = skirt!** Not "rock music" or "stone". False friend with English.
