> **Dog:** CZ pes = PL pies = RU пёс — cognate! But RU more commonly uses собака

| 1 | včera | wczoraj | gestern | вчера | yesterday |

## Connecting Words for Narration
| 🇨🇿 | 🇵🇱 | 🇩🇪 | 🇷🇺 | 🇬🇧 |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| a | i | und | и | and |
| ale | ale | aber | но | but |
| protože | ponieważ/bo | weil/denn | потому что | because |
| takže | więc/tak więc | also/deshalb | поэтому | so/therefore |
| když | kiedy/gdy | als/wenn | когда | when |
| zatímco | podczas gdy | während | пока | while |
| ačkoli | chociaż | obwohl | хотя | although |

> **CZ ale = PL ale** = but — identical!
> **CZ protože ≈ PL ponieważ** — different words but same function. PL "bo" is informal equivalent.
> **CZ když = PL gdy/kiedy** — CZ když covers both "when" and "if" in some contexts.
