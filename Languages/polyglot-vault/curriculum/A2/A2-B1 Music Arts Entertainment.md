> **Music:** CZ hudba is unique Slavic! PL muzyka = RU музыка = DE Musik — from Greek. Czech kept its own word.
> **Violin:** CZ housle ≠ PL skrzypce ≠ RU скрипка — all different words! PL+RU are onomatopoeia (from "scraping" sound).
> **Entertainment:** CZ zábava = PL zabawa — cognate! But PL zabawa also means "party/fun event."
> **Canvas:** CZ plátno = PL płótno = RU полотно — cognate! All from "plátno" (linen cloth).
