> **War/peace:** CZ válka ≠ PL wojna = RU война. PL+RU cognate but CZ uses different root. CZ mír = RU мир (also = "world"!), PL pokój (also = "room"!).
> **Weapon:** CZ zbraň ≠ PL broń. Different root, both from "bránit/bronić" (to defend)!
> **Victory:** CZ vítězství ≠ PL zwycięstwo ≠ RU победа. All three use completely different words for such a key concept.
> **Defeat:** CZ porážka = PL porażka — cognate! From "porazit/porazić" (to strike down).
> **Refugee:** CZ uprchlík (from "uprchnout"=flee), PL uchodźca (from "uchodzić"=escape), RU беженец (from "бежать"=run). All from "running away" but different roots.
