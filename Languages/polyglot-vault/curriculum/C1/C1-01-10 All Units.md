## Dialects (C1-10)
Key awareness points:
- **Polish:** Silesian, Kashubian, Podhale dialect, Warsaw vs Kraków differences
- **Czech:** Moravian dialects, Common Czech (obecná čeština) vs Literary Czech
- **German:** Hochdeutsch vs Bavarian, Swiss German, Austrian German
- **Russian:** Moscow vs St. Petersburg pronunciation, Southern dialects
- **English:** British vs American, Australian, regional UK dialects

> At C1, understanding dialectal variation shows true language mastery and cultural competence.
