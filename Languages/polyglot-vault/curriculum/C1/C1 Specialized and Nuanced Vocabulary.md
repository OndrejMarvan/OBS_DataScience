> At C1 level, ~60% of vocabulary is international (Latin/Greek) and transfers freely across ALL five languages. The remaining ~40% is native Slavic/Germanic — these are the words that require active learning.
> **Consciousness:** CZ vědomí (from vědět=know), PL świadomość (from świadom=aware), RU сознание (from знать=know). All built from "knowing" but with different prefixes!
> **Justice:** CZ spravedlnost = PL sprawiedliwość = RU справедливость — all from "pravda/prawda/правда" (truth)! "Justice = truthfulness"
