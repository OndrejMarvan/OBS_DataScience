## Cross-Linguistic Insights
1. **Bread+butter+cheese+meat+fish** = cognate across ALL Slavic: chléb/chleb/хлеб, máslo/masło/масло, sýr/ser/сыр, maso/mięso/мясо, ryba/ryba/рыба — these are core civilization words
2. **Tea etymology split:** CZ+RU got "chai" via land (Silk Road), EN+DE+PL got "te/tee/herbata" via sea (different Chinese dialect!)
3. **Beer:** CZ+PL+RU пиво/piwo/pivo from Proto-Slavic *pivo from *piti (to drink). It literally means "a drink"
4. **Apple:** jablko/jabłko/Apfel/яблоко/apple — ALL cognate across all five languages! One of the oldest shared food words in Indo-European
