## Days of the Week
| 🇨🇿 Czech | 🇵🇱 Polish | 🇩🇪 German | 🇷🇺 Russian | 🇬🇧 English | Etymology |
|-----------|------------|------------|-------------|-------------|-----------|
| pondělí | poniedziałek | Montag | понедельник | Monday | Slavic: "after Sunday"; DE+EN: Moon's day |
| úterý | wtorek | Dienstag | вторник | Tuesday | Slavic: "second" (day); EN: Tiw's day |
| středa | środa | Mittwoch | среда | Wednesday | Slavic: "middle"; DE: "mid-week"! Same concept! |
| čtvrtek | czwartek | Donnerstag | четверг | Thursday | Slavic: "fourth"; EN: Thor's day |
| pátek | piątek | Freitag | пятница | Friday | Slavic: "fifth"; EN: Freya's day |
| sobota | sobota | Samstag | суббота | Saturday | all Slavic from Hebrew "shabbat"! |
| neděle | niedziela | Sonntag | воскресенье | Sunday | CZ+PL: "not-doing" (rest day); RU: "resurrection" |

### Pattern insight
> Slavic languages count days: "after-nothing, second, middle, fourth, fifth, sabbath, no-work"
> Germanic languages name gods: Moon, Tiw, Woden, Thor, Freya, Saturn, Sun
> **středa/środa/среда = Mittwoch** — both mean "midweek"! Same logic, different families!

### Key insight
> CZ and PL use old Slavic nature-based month names — **completely different from the Latin-based names** used in DE, EN, and RU!
> CZ+PL **listopad** = "leaf-fall" = November — same word in both! 🍂
> This means months are NOT transferable between Slavic and Germanic/Russian here.

## Telling Time
| Phrase | 🇨🇿 | 🇵🇱 | 🇩🇪 | 🇷🇺 |
|--------|------|------|------|------|
| What time is it? | Kolik je hodin? | Która jest godzina? | Wie spät ist es? | Который час? |
| It's 3 o'clock | Je tři hodiny | Jest trzecia | Es ist drei Uhr | Три часа |
| half past | půl + next hour | wpół do + next hour | halb + next hour | половина + current |
| quarter past | čtvrt na + next | kwadrans po + current | Viertel nach | четверть + gen |
| quarter to | tři čtvrtě na | kwadrans do | Viertel vor | без четверти |

> ⚠️ **"Half" is confusing across languages!**
> CZ půl třetí = PL wpół do trzeciej = DE halb drei = "half TO three" = 2:30
> All three count "half toward the NEXT hour", but the phrasing is different
> EN "half past two" counts from the CURRENT hour — opposite logic!
