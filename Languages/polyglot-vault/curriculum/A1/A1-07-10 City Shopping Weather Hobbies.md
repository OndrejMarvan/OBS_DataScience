> **Blue in Russian:** RU has TWO blues — синий (dark blue) and голубой (light blue). Other languages don't distinguish!

| 12 | nerad/a + verb | nie lubię + inf | nicht gern + verb | не люблю + inf | I don't like to... |

> **"Like doing" pattern:**
> CZ: rád čtu (I gladly read)
> PL: lubię czytać (I like to-read)
> DE: ich lese gern (I read gladly)
> RU: я люблю читать (I love to-read)
> → CZ and DE use adverb "gladly", PL and RU use verb "to like/love"
