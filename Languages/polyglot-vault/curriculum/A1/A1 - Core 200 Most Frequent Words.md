## Transfer Summary for Top 200
- **CZ↔PL identical or near-identical:** ~150/200 words (75%!)
- **CZ↔RU recognizable cognate:** ~120/200 words (60%)
- **EN↔DE recognizable cognate:** ~100/200 words (50%)
- **International/shared by all 5:** ~30/200 words (15%)

> This means if you know Czech, you effectively already know **75% of the most frequent Polish words** and **60% of Russian** — you just need to learn the pronunciation and spelling differences.
