## Cross-Linguistic Insights
1. **okno** is identical in CZ, PL, and RU — a Proto-Slavic word from "oko" (eye) — a window is "the eye of the house"
2. **dveře/drzwi/дверь/Tür/door** — ALL cognate from PIE *dʰwer- — one of the oldest shared words
3. **kuchyně/kuchnia/Küche/кухня** — all from Latin "coquina" — a cultural borrowing that spread everywhere
4. **German loanwords in PL:** dach (roof), szafa (wardrobe), szuflada (drawer from Schublade)
5. **Fridge pattern:** CZ+PL both build from "ice" (led/lód → lednička/lodówka), RU from "cold" (холод → холодильник), DE from "cool-cabinet" (Kühlschrank)
