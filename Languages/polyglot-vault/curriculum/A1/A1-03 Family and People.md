## Cross-Linguistic Insights
1. **Universal family roots:** "mother", "brother", "sister" are cognate across ALL five languages — these are some of the oldest words in Indo-European
2. **Slavic precision:** Slavic languages often distinguish paternal vs maternal relatives more than Germanic (e.g., RU тесть vs свёкор)
3. **German loanwords in CZ:** švagr ← Schwager — Czech borrowed many family-related terms from German during Habsburg era
4. **"Child" plural:** CZ děti, PL dzieci, RU дети — all cognate in plural! But singulars differ (dítě/dziecko/ребёнок)
