> **God:** CZ bůh = PL bóg = RU бог — from PIE *bʰagos (giver/fortune). Also related to Sanskrit "bhaga."
> **Soul:** CZ duše = PL dusza = RU душа — identical root! From "dýchat/dychać" (to breathe). Soul = breath.
> **Sin:** CZ hřích = PL grzech = RU грех — h↔g pattern! All from Proto-Slavic *grěxъ.
> **Fate:** CZ osud ≠ PL los/przeznaczenie ≠ RU судьба — all different words! Interesting divergence for such a fundamental concept.
> **Prayer:** CZ modlitba = PL modlitwa = RU молитва — all from "modlit se/modlić się/молиться" (to pray). Cognate!
