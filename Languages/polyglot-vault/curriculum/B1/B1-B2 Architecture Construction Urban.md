> **Build:** CZ stavět ≠ PL budować — different verbs! But CZ "budova" (building) = PL "budynek" — same root "bud-" for the NOUN.
> **Bridge:** CZ most = PL most = RU мост — identical! DE Brücke + EN bridge are Germanic cognates.
> **Tower:** CZ věž = PL wieża — cognate! RU башня from Turkic. DE Turm from Latin turris.
> **Wall:** CZ stěna = PL ściana = RU стена — cognate! DE Wand = EN wall (different root).
> **Sidewalk:** CZ chodník = PL chodnik — cognate from "chodit/chodzić" (to walk). RU тротуар from French. DE Bürgersteig = "citizen-path."
> **Chimney:** CZ komín = PL komin — cognate! Both from Latin "caminus."
> **Elevator:** CZ výtah (from "vytáhnout"=pull up), PL winda (from German "Winde"=winch), DE Aufzug/Fahrstuhl, RU лифт (from EN lift). Four different origins!
