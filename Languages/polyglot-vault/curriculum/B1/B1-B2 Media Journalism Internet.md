> **Newspaper:** CZ noviny (from "nový"=new), PL gazeta (from Italian "gazzetta"), DE Zeitung (from "Zeit"=time), RU газета (same as PL). EN newspaper = "new-paper."
> **Magazine:** CZ časopis (from "čas"=time + "pis"=write = "time-writing"), PL czasopismo (same structure! "czas"=time + "pismo"=writing). Identical compound!
> **Source:** CZ zdroj ≠ PL źródło — different roots! But both mean "spring/source" (water metaphor for information source).
> **Fake news / disinformation:** Almost all languages borrowed "fake news" from English, but also have native equivalents: CZ dezinformace, PL dezinformacja, RU дезинформация — all from the same French/Latin root.
