# B1-B2 — Prefixed Verb Families

One Czech root verb + different prefixes = dozens of new verbs. The SAME prefixes work in Polish and Russian. This is one of the most efficient ways to expand your vocabulary.

## How to Use This

1. **Learn the root verb** (e.g., jít/iść/идти)
2. **Learn the prefix meanings** (při-=toward, od-=away, vy-=out, pře-=across, etc.)
3. **Combine them** — the meaning is usually predictable!
4. **Apply across languages** — the SAME prefix+root = SAME meaning in CZ, PL, and RU in ~80% of cases

> This is the most powerful vocabulary expansion technique for Slavic languages. One root + 10 prefixes = 10 new verbs. And the prefixes transfer across CZ↔PL↔RU!
