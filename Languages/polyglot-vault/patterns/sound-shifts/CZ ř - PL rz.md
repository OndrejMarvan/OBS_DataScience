# CZ ř ↔ PL rz

## Pattern Description
Czech ř [r̝] and Polish rz [ʒ] both descend from Proto-Slavic soft r' (palatalized r). Czech preserved a unique fricative trill, while Polish merged it with ż [ʒ]. In modern Polish, rz and ż are pronounced identically.

## Direction
**Proto-Slavic *r'** → CZ **ř** [r̝] / PL **rz** [ʒ] / RU **р** (plain r, palatalization lost)

## Examples
| 🇨🇿 Czech | 🇵🇱 Polish | 🇷🇺 Russian | 🇬🇧 English | Meaning |
|-----------|------------|-------------|-------------|---------|
| řeka | rzeka | река | river | river |
| dřevo | drzewo | дерево | tree/wood | wood |
| tři | trzy | три | three | three |
| řezat | rzezać | резать | to cut | to cut |
| moře | morze | море | sea | sea |
| hřib | grzyb | гриб | mushroom | mushroom |
| kříž | krzyż | крест | cross | cross |
| říkat | — | — | to say | to say |
| řád | rząd | ряд | order/row | order/government |
| před | przed | перед | before | before |
| středa | środa | среда | Wednesday | Wednesday |
| přítel | przyjaciel | приятель | friend | friend |
| břeh | brzeg | берег | shore | shore |
| dveře | drzwi | дверь | door | door |
| třeba | trzeba | — | perhaps/need | necessary |

## Exceptions / Irregularities
- PL **rząd** means "government/row" while CZ **řád** means "order/rule" — semantic drift
- Some words with CZ ř don't have rz in Polish (different root chosen)
- Russian lost the palatalization entirely, just has plain р

## Historical Note
The unique Czech ř sound [r̝] is one of the rarest sounds in world languages. Polish took the same original sound and merged it with ż. That's why Polish spelling distinguishes rz and ż even though they sound the same — rz marks etymological connection to Czech ř and Proto-Slavic r'.

## Memory Hook 🪝
**"When you see Polish rz, think Czech ř"** — they're the same letter in disguise. And Russian just dropped the whole thing and kept a simple р.

## Spelling Rule for Polish
- **rz** appears after consonants where Czech has ř: przed, trzeba, grzyb
- **ż** appears in words without Czech ř connection: żaba (žába), żona (žena uses ż not rz!)
- This helps with Polish spelling — if you know the Czech word, you know whether to write rz or ż!
