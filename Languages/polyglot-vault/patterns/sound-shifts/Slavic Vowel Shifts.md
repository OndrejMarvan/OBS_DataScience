# Slavic Vowel Shifts — CZ ↔ PL ↔ RU

## Pattern 1: CZ ou ↔ PL ó [u]
Czech diphthong "ou" corresponds to Polish "ó" (pronounced [u]).

| 🇨🇿 Czech | 🇵🇱 Polish | 🇷🇺 Russian | Meaning |
|-----------|------------|-------------|---------|
| moucha | mucha | муха | fly (insect) |
| mouka | mąka | мука | flour |
| louka | łąka | луг | meadow |
| koupit | kupić | купить | to buy |
| soud | sąd | суд | court |
| moudrý | mądry | мудрый | wise |
| dloho | długo | долго | long (time) |
| trouba | trąba | труба | trumpet/tube |

> **Memory hook:** CZ "ou" = PL "ó" = pronounced [u] in both target positions. Just different spelling for the same sound evolution!

## Pattern 2: CZ ě ↔ PL ie ↔ RU e/я

| 🇨🇿 Czech | 🇵🇱 Polish | 🇷🇺 Russian | Meaning |
|-----------|------------|-------------|---------|
| město | miasto | место | city/place |
| mléko | mleko | молоко | milk |
| svět | świat | свет | world/light |
| květina | kwiat | цветок | flower |
| věk | wiek | век | age/century |
| člověk | człowiek | человек | person |
| dědek | dziadek | дед | grandfather |
| hvězda | gwiazda | звезда | star |

> CZ ě often = PL ia/ie. The PL form often "opens" the vowel more.

## Pattern 3: CZ -ost ↔ PL -ość ↔ RU -ость

This is the most productive abstract noun suffix in all Slavic languages.

| 🇨🇿 Czech | 🇵🇱 Polish | 🇷🇺 Russian | English |
|-----------|------------|-------------|---------|
| radost | radość | радость | joy |
| starost | starość | старость | old age/worry |
| mladost | młodość | молодость | youth |
| rychlost | szybkość/prędkość | скорость | speed |
| možnost | możliwość | возможность | possibility |
| společnost | społeczność | общество | society/community |
| skutečnost | rzeczywistość | действительность | reality |
| budoucnost | przyszłość | будущность | future |
| minulost | przeszłość | прошлость | past |
| osobnost | osobowość | личность | personality |
| schopnost | zdolność | способность | ability |
| vlastnost | właściwość | свойство | property/quality |

> **This suffix is extremely productive** — you can predict new words by combining adjective + -ost/-ość/-ость:
> nový (new) → novost → nowość → новость (news/novelty)
> slavný (famous) → sláva → sława → слава (fame/glory)

## Pattern 4: Polish Nasal Vowels ↔ Czech/Russian

Polish preserved Proto-Slavic nasal vowels as ą [ɔ̃] and ę [ɛ̃]. Czech and Russian lost them.

| 🇵🇱 Polish | 🇨🇿 Czech | 🇷🇺 Russian | Meaning | What happened |
|------------|-----------|-------------|---------|--------------|
| ząb | zub | зуб | tooth | PL ą → CZ u, RU у |
| dąb | dub | дуб | oak | PL ą → CZ u, RU у |
| język | jazyk | язык | language | PL ę → CZ a, RU я |
| mąż | muž | муж | husband | PL ą → CZ u, RU у |
| mąka | mouka | мука | flour | PL ą → CZ ou, RU у |
| ręka | ruka | рука | hand | PL ę → CZ u, RU у |
| piękny | pěkný | — | pretty | PL ę → CZ ě |
| kąt | kout | кут | corner | PL ą → CZ ou, RU у |
| pięć | pět | пять | five | PL ię → CZ ě, RU я |
| dęb → dąb | dub | дуб | oak | (same entry for clarity) |

> **Key insight:** When you see Polish ą or ę, Czech usually has u/ou and Russian has у/я. This is one of the most distinctive features of Polish!
> **Memory hook:** Polish "kept its nose" (nasal vowels), Czech and Russian "lost their nose" and straightened the vowels out.

## Pattern 5: Slavic Liquid Metathesis (CZ+PL ra/la vs RU oro/olo)

| 🇨🇿 Czech | 🇵🇱 Polish | 🇷🇺 Russian | Meaning | Pattern |
|-----------|------------|-------------|---------|---------|
| hrad | gród | город | castle/city | CZ ra, PL ró, RU оро |
| brána | brama | ворота | gate | CZ rá, PL ra, RU оро |
| hlava | głowa | голова | head | CZ la, PL ło, RU оло |
| mráz | mróz | мороз | frost | CZ rá, PL ró, RU оро |
| kráva | krowa | корова | cow | CZ rá, PL ro, RU оро |
| mládí | młodość | молодость | youth | CZ lá, PL ło, RU оло |
| strana | strona | сторона | side/page | CZ ra, PL ro, RU оро |
| mlíko/mléko | mleko | молоко | milk | CZ lé, PL le, RU оло |

> **Russian "stretches out" the vowels** with the oro/olo pattern (полногласие — "full-voicing"). Czech and Polish keep it compact. This is one of the key differences between West and East Slavic!
> **Memory hook:** RU город = CZ hrad = PL gród. "гоРОд" has the "oro" expansion. Prague's "Hradčany" = "Castle district."
