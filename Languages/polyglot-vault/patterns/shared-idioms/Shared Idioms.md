# Shared Idioms Across Languages

Idioms that exist in similar form across multiple languages — learning one unlocks the others!

## Weather and Nature Idioms
| Concept | 🇨🇿 | 🇵🇱 | 🇩🇪 | 🇷🇺 | 🇬🇧 | Match? |
|---------|------|------|------|------|------|--------|
| Head in clouds | mít hlavu v oblacích | mieć głowę w chmurach | den Kopf in den Wolken haben | витать в облаках | head in the clouds | ✅ ALL |
| Raining cats & dogs | lije jako z konve | leje jak z cebra | es gießt in Strömen | льёт как из ведра | raining cats and dogs | Slavic: "pouring from bucket"; EN unique |
| Wolf in sheep's clothing | vlk v rouše beránčím | wilk w owczej skórze | Wolf im Schafspelz | волк в овечьей шкуре | wolf in sheep's clothing | ✅ ALL (biblical) |
| Kill two birds, one stone | zabít dvě mouchy jednou ranou | upiec dwie pieczenie na jednym ogniu | zwei Fliegen mit einer Klappe schlagen | убить двух зайцев одним выстрелом | kill two birds with one stone | Different animals in each! |

## Body Idioms
| Concept | 🇨🇿 | 🇵🇱 | 🇩🇪 | 🇷🇺 | 🇬🇧 |
|---------|------|------|------|------|------|
| Know like back of hand | znát jako své boty | znać jak własną kieszeń | wie seine Westentasche kennen | знать как свои пять пальцев | know like the back of my hand |
| Cross fingers | držet palce | trzymać kciuki | Daumen drücken | держать кулаки | cross fingers |
| Have butterflies | mít motýly v břiše | mieć motyle w brzuchu | Schmetterlinge im Bauch haben | бабочки в животе | butterflies in stomach |

> **"Cross fingers" pattern:** CZ+PL+DE all say "hold/press thumbs" — only English actually crosses fingers! RU "holds fists" (кулаки).

## How to Use This File
1. When you learn an idiom in one language, check if it exists in the others
2. Mark ones that are identical (easy to transfer) vs unique (need to memorize separately)
3. The ones that differ the MOST are the most interesting — they reveal cultural differences
