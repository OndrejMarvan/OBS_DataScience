## Suffix Pattern — How International Words Adapt
| EN suffix | PL | DE | RU | CZ |
|-----------|----|----|----|----|
| -tion | -cja | -tion | -ция | -ce/-cie |
| -ment | -ment | -ment | -мент | -ment |
| -ism | -izm | -ismus | -изм | -ismus/-izmus |
| -ist | -ista | -ist | -ист | -ista |
| -ology | -ologia | -ologie | -ология | -ologie |
| -ic | -iczny | -isch | -ический | -ický |
| -al | -alny | -al/-ell | -альный | -ální |

> **This suffix table is GOLD.** Once you know the pattern, you can predict how ANY international word adapts to each language. For example: EN "globalization" → PL globalizacja, DE Globalisierung, RU глобализация, CZ globalizace.

## How Many Words Is This?
Conservatively, you have **300-500 international words** that are essentially free across all five languages. At B2-C1 level, these make up a significant chunk of academic and professional vocabulary.
