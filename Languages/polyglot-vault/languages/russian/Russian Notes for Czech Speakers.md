# Russian-Specific Notes for Czech Speakers

## 1. Mobile Stress — The Hardest Part of Russian

Russian stress is UNPREDICTABLE and can move within a word's paradigm. Czech has fixed first-syllable stress. This is your biggest challenge.

### Stress can change meaning!
| Word | Stress 1 | Meaning | Stress 2 | Meaning |
|------|----------|---------|----------|---------|
| замок | зА́мок | castle | замО́к | lock |
| мука | мУ́ка | torture | мукА́ | flour |
| писать | писА́ть | to write | пИ́сать | to pee (vulgar!) |
| уже | уже́ | already | У́же | narrower |
| дорога | дорО́га | road | дорогА́ | dear (f) |
| село | селО́ | village | сЕ́ло | sat down (past, n) |

### Common stress patterns
| Pattern | Example | Paradigm |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Fixed stem stress | кнИ́га → кнИ́ги → кнИ́ге → кнИ́гу | stays on stem |
| Fixed ending stress | странА́ → странЫ́ → странЕ́ → странУ́ | stays on ending |
| Moving stress | головА́ → гО́ловы (pl!) | shifts in plural |
| Moving stress | окнО́ → О́кна (pl!) | shifts in plural |
| Verb stress shift | писА́ть → пишУ́ → пИ́шешь → пИ́шет | shifts in conjugation |

### Strategy for Czech speakers
- **Learn stress with every word** — mark it in your notes (ударение)
- Many CZ-RU cognates have DIFFERENT stress: CZ **vo**da → RU во**да́**, CZ **mě**sto → RU ме**сто́**
- Russian unstressed О sounds like [a]! молокО́ = [малакО́]

## 2. Vowel Reduction (аканье)

In Russian, unstressed vowels change sound:
- **О** → [a] when unstressed: хорошо́ = [харашО́], молоко́ = [малакО́]
- **Е** → [и] when unstressed: весна́ = [висна́]
- **Я** → [и] when unstressed: язы́к = [изЫ́к]

> This doesn't happen in Czech or Polish! All vowels keep their quality regardless of stress. This is a major listening challenge.

## 3. No "to be" in Present Tense!

| CZ | PL | RU | EN |
|----|----|----|----|
| Jsem student. | Jestem studentem. | Я студент. (NO verb!) | I am a student. |
| To je dům. | To jest dom. | Это дом. (NO verb!) | This is a house. |
| Ona je učitelka. | Ona jest nauczycielką. | Она учительница. | She is a teacher. |

> Russian DROPS "есть" (to be) in present tense. Past and future bring it back:
> Я **был** студентом. (I was a student.)
> Я **буду** студентом. (I will be a student.)

## 4. Aspect Differences from Czech

While the SYSTEM is the same, some specific verb pairs differ:

| Meaning | CZ impf/perf | RU impf/perf | Notes |
|---------|-------------|-------------|-------|
| to put (lay) | klást/položit | класть/положить | identical logic! |
| to take | brát/vzít | брать/взять | identical! |
| to speak | mluvit/promluvit | говорить/сказать | RU uses DIFFERENT root for perf! |
| to go (perf) | jít → přijít | идти → прийти | same prefix logic |
| to buy | kupovat/koupit | покупать/купить | RU adds по- prefix |
| to see | vidět/uvidět | видеть/увидеть | identical! |

## 5. Cases — Minor Differences from Czech

| Feature | Czech | Russian | Impact |
|---------|-------|---------|--------|
| Vocative | ✅ Full case (Petře! Mami!) | ❌ Mostly gone | One less case to learn! |
| Locative name | Lokál | Предложный (prepositional) | Always needs a preposition in RU |
| Animate accusative | Masc sg only | Masc sg + ALL animate plural | Wider scope in Russian |
| Genitive of negation | Sometimes | More frequent | У меня нет **времени** (gen) |

## 6. Tricky Cyrillic Letters

Letters that LOOK like Latin but sound DIFFERENT:

| Cyrillic | Looks like | Actually sounds like | Example |
|----------|-----------|---------------------|---------|
| **В** в | B | V [v] | Волга = Volga (not Bolga!) |
| **Н** н | H | N [n] | Нет = Net (not Het!) |
| **Р** р | P | R [r] | Россия = Rossiya (not Possiya!) |
| **С** с | C | S [s] | Москва = Moskva (not Mockva!) |
| **У** у | Y | U [u] | Утро = Utro |
| **Х** х | X | Kh [x] | Хлеб = Khleb |

### Letters that don't exist in Latin
| Cyrillic | Sound | Closest CZ/PL |
|----------|-------|---------------|
| **Ы** ы | hard [ɨ] | harder than CZ y |
| **Э** э | pure [e] | CZ é without softening |
| **Ю** ю | [ju] | CZ ju |
| **Я** я | [ja] | CZ ja |
| **Ъ** ъ | hard sign | no sound — separates |
| **Ь** ь | soft sign | no sound — softens previous consonant |
| **Щ** щ | [ɕː] long soft sh | PL szcz! |

## 7. Handwriting (Прописные буквы)

Russian handwritten letters look VERY different from printed ones. Key surprises:

| Printed | Handwritten | Looks like |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| Т т | т (cursive) | looks like "m" |
| Д д | д (cursive) | looks like "g" |
| И и | и (cursive) | looks like "u" |
| Л л | л (cursive) | looks like a hook |
| П п | п (cursive) | looks like "n" |

> If you plan to read Russian handwriting, practice separately — it's almost a different script!
