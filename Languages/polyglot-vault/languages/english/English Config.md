# 🇬🇧 English — Language Configuration

## Language Info
- **Language:** English
- **ISO Code:** en
- **Language Family:** Indo-European → Germanic → West Germanic → Anglo-Frisian
- **Script:** Latin (no diacritics)
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Role in vault:** Bridge to Germanic family, source of international vocabulary

## Role in Your Learning System

English serves two main functions:

### 1. Bridge to German
English and German are siblings in the Germanic family. Knowing English gives you a massive head start with German vocabulary:
- water/Wasser, house/Haus, book/Buch, hand/Hand, finger/Finger
- The High German consonant shift explains most differences systematically

### 2. Source of International Vocabulary
Latin/Greek-derived words in English appear across ALL your languages:
- EN democracy → PL demokracja → DE Demokratie → RU демократия → CZ demokracie
- EN university → PL uniwersytet → DE Universität → RU университет → CZ univerzita
- EN information → PL informacja → DE Information → RU информация → CZ informace
