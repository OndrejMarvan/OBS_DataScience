# 🇩🇪 Path — German (A2 rebuild → B2)

**Your situation:** You were A2 once; the knowledge is dormant, not dead. Rebuilding is 3× faster than first learning. Your assets: English (Germanic sibling = free vocabulary), Czech (case-system intuition, first-syllable stress, hundreds of shared loanwords). Your enemies: articles, V2/verb-final word order, separable verbs.

**Your goal:** Reactivate to solid A2 in ~1 month, then climb to B2 where German media becomes enjoyable.

## Phase 1 — Reactivation (weeks 1-4)
Don't start from zero — *sweep* through what you knew:
1. Read [[German Config]] — refresh the mental map, especially the loanword list (words you already know via Czech: šunka→Schinken, švagr→Schwager)
2. Read [[EN-DE High German Consonant Shift]] — this converts your English vocabulary into German guesses (water→Wasser, make→machen). Highest-leverage 20 minutes in this vault for German.
3. Sweep [[A1 - Core 200 Most Frequent Words]] — read ONLY the DE column, mark anything you don't instantly recognize, make word notes for those only
4. Grammar re-anchor: [[German Articles Guide]] (accept it: learn every noun WITH der/die/das from now on) + [[Cases - Cross-Linguistic Comparison]] (German = your Czech cases, simplified to 4)

**Input:** slow-German podcasts or Nicos Weg (free, A1-B1, story-based). 15 min/day beats 2 hours on Sunday.

## Phase 2 — The word-order war (months 2-4)
This is where German is genuinely different from everything you know:
1. [[Word Order Comparison]] — V2 rule + verb-final subordinate clauses. Read it, then **drill it in output**: every writing exercise, check verb position first.
2. [[German Separable Verbs]] — the 20 essential ones. These are un-Czech; they need explicit practice.
3. [[Reported Speech and Complex Sentences]] — the weil/dass/wenn verb-final section
4. Keep input daily; graduate to "Nachrichten in einfacher Sprache" (simplified news)

**Output from week 5:** write 3 sentences daily. The ONLY self-check: is the verb in position 2 (main) or final (sub-clause)? One habit at a time.

## Phase 3 — B1 → B2 climb (months 4-10)
- [[German Konjunktiv and Relative Clauses]] — würde/hätte/wäre unlocks polite, hypothetical, nuanced German
- Vocabulary: work through [[Vocabulary Index by Theme]] DE columns for topics you actually talk about; log unknowns
- Input: real German — Tagesschau, German YouTube in your interest areas, TV series with DE subtitles
- Output: weekly longer text or 2-min recording; conversation practice if at all possible

## Traps specific to you
| Trap | Fix |
|------|-----|
| Ignoring articles ("I'll learn them later") | You won't. der/die/das WITH every noun, from day 1 of the rebuild. |
| Czech word order in German sentences | Verb position check on every written sentence, every time |
| Assuming CZ/PL gender predicts DE gender | It's right only ~45% — worse than useful. See [[German Articles Guide]] |
| False friends via English | Gift=poison, bekommen=receive, aktuell=current → [[False Friends Extended]] |
| No aspect in German feels "imprecise" | Use adverbs (gerade, schon, immer wieder) — that's how German does it |
