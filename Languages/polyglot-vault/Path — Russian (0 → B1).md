# 🇷🇺 Path — Russian (0 → B1)

**Your situation:** True beginner — but the best-positioned beginner possible. Czech gives you ~60% vocabulary recognition, the entire case/aspect system, and free grammar intuition. Your only *real* barriers: Cyrillic (2 weeks), mobile stress (ongoing), and vowel reduction in listening.

**Your goal:** Reading Cyrillic fluently in a month, A2 in ~4-5 months, B1 within a year — realistic *for you specifically* because you skip 70% of what Western learners struggle with.

## Phase 0 — Cyrillic bootcamp (weeks 1-2) 🔑
Nothing else matters until this is automatic:
1. [[Russian Config]] — the Cyrillic↔Czech letter map. 10 min/day.
2. Trap letters first: **В=V, Н=N, Р=R, С=S** (they lie to your Latin-trained eyes)
3. Daily drill: read Russian cognates aloud — вода, брат, сестра, молоко, три. You *know* these words; you're only training the script.
4. Type, don't just read: install a RU keyboard (phonetic layout maps to your habits), type 5 words daily.

**Done when:** you can read a simple sentence without letter-by-letter decoding.

## Phase 1 — Cognate harvest (months 1-3)
Exploit your unfair advantage:
1. [[A1 - Core 200 Most Frequent Words]] — RU column. ~60% you'll recognize via Czech instantly; word-note the rest.
2. [[Slavic Cognate Master Patterns]] + [[CZ h - PL g - RU г]] + [[Slavic Vowel Shifts]] (the oro/olo section: hrad→город, hlava→голова) — these convert thousands of Czech words into Russian recognition
3. Grammar freebies to read once (you already *know* this, just see the RU forms): [[Cases - Cross-Linguistic Comparison]], [[Aspect Comparison]], [[Negation Comparison]]
4. Key structural surprise: **no "to be" in present** — я студент. Read [[Russian Notes for Czech Speakers]].
5. ⚠️ [[False Friends Master List]] RU section: čas≠час, město≠место, ovoce≠овощи, rodina≠родина

**Input:** comprehensible-input beginner videos/podcasts (slow Russian with visuals). Даже 10 min/day.

## Phase 2 — Stress and listening (months 3-8)
The genuinely hard part for Czechs:
1. **Mobile stress:** from now on, never note a Russian word without marking its stress (мОжно vs можнО changes everything). [[Russian Notes for Czech Speakers]] has the patterns.
2. **Vowel reduction:** unstressed о = [a] (молоко = malakó). Train ears with listening, not reading — audio-first learning from here.
3. [[Verbs of Motion]] — RU has the most elaborate system; your CZ intuition covers 80%
4. Vocabulary: [[B1 - Core Verbs Top 100]] RU column + themed files as topics arise
5. Output starts: write 3 sentences/day in Cyrillic; speak along with slow audio (shadowing)

## Phase 3 — Toward B1 (months 8-12+)
- Real input: Russian YouTube with RU subtitles, films you know from Czech dubs, easy books
- [[B1-B2 Prefixed Verb Families]] — the на-/вы-/пере- prefixes match Czech almost 1:1; this explodes your verb count
- Conversation practice becomes essential — tandem or tutor
- Precision files: [[B2-C1 Connectors and Discourse Markers]] RU column

## Traps specific to you
| Trap | Fix |
|------|-----|
| Skipping Cyrillic drills ("I can decode slowly") | Slow decoding blocks everything downstream. Two focused weeks, done forever. |
| Czech first-syllable stress | Mark stress on EVERY word note, no exceptions |
| Reading-only learning | RU spelling hides pronunciation (reduction). Audio must be ≥50% of input. |
| Trusting look-alikes | живот=belly, неделя=week. Check false friends. |
| Writing print letters when natives write cursive | Fine for now — cursive is a Phase 3 luxury, don't stress it |
