# Negation — Cross-Linguistic Comparison

## Basic Negation

| Language | Method | Example |
|----------|--------|---------|
| 🇨🇿 | **ne** + verb (fused) | **Ne**vím. **Ne**rozumím. |
| 🇵🇱 | **nie** + verb (separate) | **Nie** wiem. **Nie** rozumiem. |
| 🇩🇪 | **nicht** (end of clause) | Ich weiß **nicht**. Ich verstehe **nicht**. |
| 🇷🇺 | **не** + verb (separate) | Я **не** знаю. Я **не** понимаю. |
| 🇬🇧 | **do not** + verb | I **don't** know. I **don't** understand. |

> **CZ+PL+RU:** Negation particle goes BEFORE the verb — same logic!
> CZ fuses it (nevím), PL and RU keep it separate (nie wiem, не знаю).
> **DE:** nicht goes to the END — completely different placement!
> **EN:** Needs auxiliary "do" — most complex system.

## Double/Multiple Negation

**Slavic languages USE double negation — it's correct and required!**
**English considers double negation incorrect. German is in between.**

| 🇨🇿 | 🇵🇱 | 🇷🇺 | 🇬🇧 literal | 🇬🇧 correct |
|------|------|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Nikdy** **nic** **ne**řekl. | **Nigdy** **nic** **nie** powiedział. | Он **никогда** **ничего** **не** сказал. | He never nothing didn't say. | He never said anything. |
| **Nikdo** **nic** **ne**ví. | **Nikt** **nic** **nie** wie. | **Никто** **ничего** **не** знает. | Nobody nothing doesn't know. | Nobody knows anything. |
| **Nikam** **ne**jdu. | **Nigdzie** **nie** idę. | **Никуда** **не** иду. | I'm not going nowhere. | I'm not going anywhere. |

> **KEY RULE for Slavic:** When you have negative pronouns (nikdo/nikt/никто, nic/nic/ничего, nikdy/nigdy/никогда), you STILL negate the verb too! This is MANDATORY in all three.

|---------|------|------|------|------|------|

> nikdo/nikt/никто, nic/nic/ничего, nikdy/nigdy/никогда — beautifully regular!

## Negation of "to have" and "to be"

### "There is no..." / "I don't have..."
| 🇨🇿 | 🇵🇱 | 🇩🇪 | 🇷🇺 | 🇬🇧 |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| Nemám čas. | Nie mam czasu. | Ich habe keine Zeit. | У меня нет времени. | I don't have time. |
| Není tu nikdo. | Nie ma tu nikogo. | Es ist niemand da. | Здесь никого нет. | There's nobody here. |
| Nebyl jsem tam. | Nie byłem tam. | Ich war nicht dort. | Меня там не было. | I wasn't there. |

> ⚠️ **Genitive of negation:** In CZ+PL+RU, negated "have" often takes GENITIVE instead of accusative!
> CZ: Mám **čas** (acc) → Nemám **času** (gen)
> PL: Mam **czas** (acc) → Nie mam **czasu** (gen)
> RU: У меня есть **время** (nom) → У меня нет **времени** (gen)
> DE and EN don't do this at all.

## "Not yet" / "No longer"

| Concept | 🇨🇿 | 🇵🇱 | 🇩🇪 | 🇷🇺 | 🇬🇧 |
|---------|------|------|------|------|------|
| not yet | ještě ne | jeszcze nie | noch nicht | ещё нет/не | not yet |
| no longer | už ne | już nie | nicht mehr | уже нет/не | no longer |
| not at all | vůbec ne | wcale nie | gar nicht/überhaupt nicht | совсем не | not at all |

> CZ+PL identical: ještě ne / jeszcze nie, už ne / już nie, vůbec ne / wcale nie!
