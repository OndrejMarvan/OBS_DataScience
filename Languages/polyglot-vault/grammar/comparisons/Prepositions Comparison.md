### Key Patterns
> **CZ ≈ PL in almost everything!** Same prepositions, same cases in 90% of cases:
> - v+loc = w+loc ✅
> - z+gen = z+gen ✅ 
> - s+ins = z+ins ⚠️ (different preposition! CZ "s" = PL "z" for "with")
> - do+gen = do+gen ✅
> - od+gen = od+gen ✅
> - na+loc/acc = na+loc/acc ✅

> ⚠️ **The big CZ↔PL trap:** CZ **s** (with) = PL **z** (with). But CZ **z** (from) = PL **z** (from) too! Polish "z" does double duty.

> **DE is simpler** — only 4 cases to worry about, and many prepositions that take loc/ins in Slavic just take dative in German.
