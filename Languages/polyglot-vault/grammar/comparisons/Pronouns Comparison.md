# Personal Pronouns — Cross-Linguistic Comparison

## Subject Pronouns (Nominative)

| Person | 🇨🇿 | 🇵🇱 | 🇩🇪 | 🇷🇺 | 🇬🇧 |
|--------|------|------|------|------|------|
| I | já | ja | ich | я | I |
| you (sg inf) | ty | ty | du | ты | you |
| he | on | on | er | он | he |
| she | ona | ona | sie | она | she |
| it | ono | ono | es | оно | it |
| we | my | my | wir | мы | we |
| you (pl/formal) | vy | wy | ihr / Sie | вы | you |
| they (masc) | oni | oni | sie | они | they |
| they (fem/neut) | ony | one | sie | они | they |

> **CZ = PL** in almost every form: já/ja, ty/ty, on/on, ona/ona, my/my, oni/oni. Nearly identical!
> **RU** is also very close: я, ты, он, она, мы, они
> **"They":** CZ+PL distinguish masculine-personal (oni) vs other (ony/one). DE+RU+EN don't.

## Accusative (direct object)

| Person | 🇨🇿 | 🇵🇱 | 🇩🇪 | 🇷🇺 |
|--------|------|------|------|------|
| me | mě/mne | mnie | mich | меня |
| you | tě/tebe | ciebie/cię | dich | тебя |
| him | ho/jeho/něho | go/jego/niego | ihn | его |
| her | ji | ją | sie | её |
| us | nás | nas | uns | нас |
| you (pl) | vás | was | euch | вас |
| them | je | ich | sie | их |

> CZ nás/vás = PL nas/was = RU нас/вас — almost identical in plural forms!

## Dative (indirect object)

| Person | 🇨🇿 | 🇵🇱 | 🇩🇪 | 🇷🇺 |
|--------|------|------|------|------|
| to me | mi/mně | mi/mnie | mir | мне |
| to you | ti/tobě | ci/tobie | dir | тебе |
| to him | mu/jemu | mu/jemu | ihm | ему |
| to her | jí | jej | ihr | ей |
| to us | nám | nam | uns | нам |
| to you (pl) | vám | wam | euch | вам |
| to them | jim | im | ihnen | им |

> CZ mi = PL mi, CZ mu = PL mu, CZ nám = PL nam, CZ vám = PL wam — nearly IDENTICAL!
> RU is also very close: мне, тебе, ему, нам, вам, им

## Possessive Pronouns

| | 🇨🇿 | 🇵🇱 | 🇩🇪 | 🇷🇺 |
|--|------|------|------|------|
| my | můj/má/mé | mój/moja/moje | mein/e | мой/моя/моё |
| your (sg) | tvůj/tvá/tvé | twój/twoja/twoje | dein/e | твой/твоя/твоё |
| his | jeho | jego | sein/e | его |
| her | její | jej | ihr/e | её |
| our | náš/naše | nasz/nasza/nasze | unser/e | наш/наша/наше |
| your (pl) | váš/vaše | wasz/wasza/wasze | euer/eure | ваш/ваша/ваше |
| their | jejich | ich | ihr/e | их |

> **CZ můj = PL mój = RU мой** — same word! Same for tvůj/twój/твой, náš/nasz/наш, váš/wasz/ваш.
> **DE possessives** decline like articles — need case endings (meinen, meinem, meines, etc.)

## Reflexive Pronouns

| | 🇨🇿 | 🇵🇱 | 🇩🇪 | 🇷🇺 |
|--|------|------|------|------|
| Reflexive (acc) | se | się | sich | себя/-ся |
| Reflexive (dat) | si | sobie | sich | себе |

> CZ se/si = PL się/sobie — same system!
> DE "sich" covers both accusative and dative — simpler.
> RU has both full form (себя/себе) and suffix form (-ся/-сь on verbs).
