# German Separable and Inseparable Verbs

## The Concept
German has verbs where the prefix separates from the verb in main clauses. This doesn't exist in Czech, Polish, or Russian — it's uniquely Germanic.

## How It Works
**aufstehen** (to get up) = **auf** + stehen

| Sentence type | Example | What happens |
|--------------|---------|-------------|
| Main clause | Ich **stehe** um 7 Uhr **auf**. | Prefix goes to END |
| Question | **Stehst** du früh **auf**? | Prefix stays at end |
| Subordinate | ..., weil ich früh **aufstehe**. | Prefix REATTACHES! |
| Infinitive | Ich möchte früh **aufstehen**. | Together as one word |
| Past participle | Ich bin **auf**ge**standen**. | ge- goes BETWEEN! |

## Common Separable Prefixes

| Prefix | Meaning | Example verb | Meaning |
|--------|---------|-------------|---------|
| **an-** | on, at | anrufen | to call (phone) |
| **auf-** | up, open | aufmachen | to open |
| **aus-** | out | ausgehen | to go out |
| **ein-** | in | einladen | to invite |
| **mit-** | with, along | mitkommen | to come along |
| **nach-** | after | nachdenken | to think about |
| **vor-** | before, forward | vorstellen | to introduce |
| **zu-** | closed, to | zumachen | to close |
| **zurück-** | back | zurückkommen | to come back |
| **ab-** | off, down | abfahren | to depart |
| **um-** | around | umziehen | to move (house) |
| **weg-** | away | weggehen | to go away |
| **fest-** | firm | feststellen | to determine |
| **fern-** | far | fernsehen | to watch TV |
| **hin-** | there (away) | hingehen | to go there |
| **her-** | here (toward) | herkommen | to come here |

## Inseparable Prefixes (NEVER separate)

| Prefix | Example | Meaning | Past participle |
|--------|---------|---------|----------------|
| **be-** | bekommen | to receive | **be**kommen (no ge-!) |
| **emp-** | empfehlen | to recommend | **emp**fohlen |
| **ent-** | entschuldigen | to excuse | **ent**schuldigt |
| **er-** | erklären | to explain | **er**klärt |
| **ge-** | gehören | to belong | **ge**hört |
| **miss-** | missverstehen | to misunderstand | **miss**verstanden |
| **ver-** | verstehen | to understand | **ver**standen |
| **zer-** | zerstören | to destroy | **zer**stört |

> **Memory trick:** Inseparable prefixes are UNSTRESSED. Separable prefixes are STRESSED.
> **auf**stehen (STRESS on auf → separable)
> ver**steh**en (stress on steh → inseparable)

## Slavic Analogy
While Czech/Polish don't separate prefixes, the PREFIX MEANINGS are often similar:

| DE prefix | CZ prefix | PL prefix | Shared meaning |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| aus- (out) | vy- | wy- | out |
| ein- (in) | v- | w- | in |
| auf- (up/open) | ot(e)- / roz- | ot(w)- / roz- | open |
| zu- (closed) | za- | za- | close |
| mit- (with) | s(e)- / spolu- | z(e)- / współ- | with/together |
| zurück- (back) | zpět/zpátky | z powrotem | back |
| ab- (off/away) | od- | od- | away/off |
| um- (around) | pře-/ob- | prze-/ob- | around/re- |

> So "aufmachen" (auf=open + machen=do) is like CZ "otevřít" (ote-=open + vřít) — the logic is the same, the execution is different!
